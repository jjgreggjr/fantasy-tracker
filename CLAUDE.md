# Working in this repo

## Keep answers short

James wants short answers, for fantasy questions and repo work alike:

- Lead with the decision or result in one line.
- Then at most 3–5 bullets or one small table: only what drove the call and what would change it.
- No preamble, no restating the question, no recap of steps you took unless he asks.
- Offer depth ("want the full breakdown?") instead of writing it.

For fantasy questions, `skills/fantasy/SKILL.md` has the rest of the rules (status checks, points with volume).
